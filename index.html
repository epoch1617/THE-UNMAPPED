I · The Territory

A map that maps itself.

Most puzzles give you a problem and wait for you to solve it.

This one gives you a territory. The territory is real. The borders shift depending on where you stand. Some regions only appear if you look away from them.

The cartographer left six fragments. Five are visible. One requires passage through the final gate.

Read the fragments in any order. The territory does not care about sequence. Only about depth of entry.

II · The Six Fragments

What the cartographer left behind.

The First Country
"I named a country once. The country did not survive the naming. This taught me something about names."
The Instrument
"Every instrument of measurement changes what it measures. The compass magnetises the thing it points at."
The Border Problem
"A border is an argument two territories are having. The cartographer does not take sides. He draws the argument."
The Unmappable
"There is one territory I never mapped. Not because I couldn't find it. Because once you map it, it moves."
The Legend
"The legend of a map explains the symbols. What explains the legend? I have been asking this for forty years."
